RemiBloom

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short: RemiBloom keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. The app does not send your reminders or any other personal data to us or to anyone else.

This policy explains what the RemiBloom app for Android (“the app”) does with information. If you have questions, email samhitha.apps@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into the app is saved only in the app’s storage on your phone:

  • your reminders, checklists, repeats and skipped days;
  • birthdays, anniversaries and other special days;
  • renewals and subscriptions (only the type, a name you choose, who it’s for and the date; the app never asks for document numbers or photos);
  • your settings, such as text size, background, sounds and check-in time.

We have no servers that receive this information, and we cannot see it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect personal data, does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, does not show ads, and does not use the advertising ID.

Contacts

If you choose to add someone to a reminder (for example “Call Mom”), the app asks for permission to read your contacts and shows you a list to pick from. Only the name and phone number of the person you pick are saved, on your phone, so the reminder can open your phone’s dialer, Messages or WhatsApp. Contacts are never uploaded or shared.

Notifications and alarms

The app asks to send notifications, schedule exact alarms and show full-screen alarms so reminders arrive on time, including when the phone is locked. These are handled by Android on your phone.

Purchases

RemiBloom Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card, UPI or other payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account has Premium. Google’s handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups

The app can save a backup file of your reminders and settings. You choose where that file goes (for example your phone’s files, Google Drive or an email to yourself). The app does not upload it anywhere by itself.

Android may also back up app data to your own Google account if you have turned on backup in your phone’s settings. That is done by Android and Google, not by RemiBloom, and you can control it in your phone’s settings.

Feedback emails

If you tap “Send feedback”, the app opens your own email app with a message to us. It includes your phone model, Android version and app version so we can fix problems; you can see and change everything before you send it. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to reply and to improve the app. We will delete your emails on request.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them (or from anyone).

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your phone, you are in control: delete reminders in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Backup files you saved yourself stay wherever you put them until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
